engineering notes — the fusion that made things worse

Measuring before believing. Reciprocal Rank Fusion is the standard move for combining keyword and vector retrieval, and it is widely assumed to help. On this corpus it didn't: applied unweighted, it pulled MRR below what one of its own input rankers scored alone. Without the eval harness I would have shipped it and called it an improvement.

Routing instead of blending. Different queries want different retrievers — an exact symbol name wants keyword matching, a "how does X work" question wants embeddings. A per-query router picks one rather than averaging both into mush, which is what took MRR to 0.55.

engineering notes — the stablecoins that were always anomalous

Why z-score alone fails. A stablecoin's price barely moves, so its standard deviation is tiny, and any normal tick registers as a huge deviation. Pure z-score detection flagged them constantly. Requiring both a statistical outlier and a 2% absolute move killed the false positives without weakening detection on volatile assets.

The column that was too small. A String(20) field looked generous until real asset identifiers arrived longer than that and writes started failing. Schema limits picked from the examples in front of you are guesses, and they fail on the data you haven't seen yet.
